About Photominac

what it does, and why it's built differently

Most photo apps ask you to trade privacy for convenience, or organization for storage. Photominac keeps the three in balance: everything is grouped automatically, nothing leaves your session without your say, and albums build themselves while you keep full control.

How it compares

Using Photominac

  1. Import your photos.

    Open Import from your profile menu, drop in a batch of images, and tag them with a category — or leave them uncategorized and sort later.

  2. Let albums build themselves.

    Photominac keeps a rotating "Picked for you" album automatically. Build your own by naming it and tapping the photos that belong.

  3. Browse the gallery.

    Filter by category, hover to preview, and click any photo to open it full-screen with the lightbox — arrow through the set with the on-screen controls or your keyboard.

  4. Revisit your memories.

    Every import is grouped by week, month and year automatically, with an "on this day" flashback surfaced at the top each time you visit.
