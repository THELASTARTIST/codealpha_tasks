About the four instruments

Every calculator on this panel targets a different kind of problem. Below is a quick reference for what each one is built for, how they differ, and roughly how much they can do.

Basic

Handles everyday math — addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentages. Found in pocket devices and till receipts, built for speed over depth.

Scientific

Adds trigonometry, logarithms, exponents and statistical functions. Used by students and engineers to solve multi-step formulas by hand.

Graphing

Plots functions visually, solves simultaneous equations, and handles matrices on a larger screen. Standard equipment in high school and college math classes.

Financial

Purpose-built for business and accounting: loan amortization, cash flow, interest rates and compound interest calculations.
