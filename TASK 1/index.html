                Why one player beats four apps

                How to use it

                1. Link one identity. Sign in with Google and connect Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube Music — one at a time, so your history stays untangled.
                2. Import the rest. Drop in local files from anywhere else your music lives — purchased tracks, DJ sets, voice memos, whatever isn't on a streaming service.
                3. Queue across sources. Build one queue that mixes imported files and your connected playlists. Shuffle, repeat, and autoplay all work across the whole queue, not just one app's slice of it.
                4. Keep the deck open. The now-playing bar stays fixed at the bottom while you browse, search, or read — nothing interrupts playback.

                Why it's different

                Most listening apps are built to keep you inside one catalog. AUDIOPROCTOR is built around the fact that your actual music lives in four or five different places — a streaming subscription, an old MP3 folder, a friend's SoundCloud rip, a voice memo of a demo. Instead of forcing you to juggle apps, it gives every source the same player, the same queue, and the same controls.
